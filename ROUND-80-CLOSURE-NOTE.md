# Round 80 Closure Note

Round 80 is reserved for exact-final-PR-head and merged-main evidence closure. Any quality, Baseline Archive Integrity, regression, static, package-parity or release-identity failure discovered at this stage is classified as a Round-80 defect and must be corrected and rerun before merge/completion. If both exact PR-head gates and both merged-main gates pass without a new defect, Round 80 is classified NO DEFECT.
